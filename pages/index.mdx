# Gabriel Noya

Hey! I'm an Electrical Engineering and Computer Science undergrad at Stanford University, planning to graduate in June 2026.

My current interests are foundation models for sequential and structured data (think next-token prediction, but the tokens are financial transactions), RL environments and benchmarks for frontier models, AI agents for reverse engineering and binary analysis (using agents to crack games bypass Denuvo), and the intersection of world models with classical simulation engines for robotics.

Previously I worked on GPU-based robotics simulation at NVIDIA and on using LLMs for chip design at Stanford.

Please reach out if any of these ideas sound interesting to you, and check out the other pages to learn more about me!
